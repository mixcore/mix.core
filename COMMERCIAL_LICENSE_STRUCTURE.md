# MIXCORE COMMERCIAL LICENSE STRUCTURE

## OVERVIEW

Mixcore offers several commercial license options to accommodate organizations of different sizes, revenue levels, and use cases worldwide. Each license tier provides specific exemptions from the Mixcore Source Available License restrictions.

## LICENSE TIERS

### 1. ATTRIBUTION REMOVAL LICENSE

**Purpose**: Removes only the "powered by Mixcore CMS" attribution requirement while keeping Commons Clause restrictions.

**Pricing Structure**:
- **Startup**: $499/year (annual revenue under $1M)
- **Growth**: $999/year (annual revenue $1M-$10M)
- **Enterprise**: $1,999/year (annual revenue over $10M)

**Benefits**:
- Remove all "powered by Mixcore CMS" attributions
- Basic email support (response within 48 hours)
- Access to attribution removal documentation
- Applicable for single deployment only

### 2. STANDARD COMMERCIAL LICENSE

**Purpose**: Removes attribution requirements and permits commercial use including SaaS offerings for a single deployment.

**Pricing Structure**:
- **Startup**: $1,499/year (annual revenue under $1M)
- **Growth**: $2,999/year (annual revenue $1M-$10M)
- **Enterprise**: $5,999/year (annual revenue over $10M)

**Benefits**:
- All Attribution Removal License benefits
- Exemption from Commons Clause restrictions
- Priority email support (response within 24 hours)
- Access to premium documentation
- One deployment for internal or external use

### 3. AGENCY LICENSE

**Purpose**: Designed for web development agencies creating sites for multiple clients.

**Pricing Structure**:
- **Boutique Agency**: $2,999/year (up to 5 client projects)
- **Mid-size Agency**: $5,999/year (up to 15 client projects)
- **Large Agency**: $9,999/year (up to 30 client projects)
- **Enterprise Agency**: Custom pricing (unlimited client projects)

**Benefits**:
- All Standard Commercial License benefits
- Multiple client deployment rights (according to tier)
- Dedicated support contact
- Quarterly review meetings
- Access to agency implementation guides

### 4. OEM/SAAS LICENSE

**Purpose**: For organizations embedding Mixcore in their own products or offering it as a service.

**Pricing Structure**:
- **Startup OEM**: $4,999/year (annual revenue under $1M)
- **Growth OEM**: $9,999/year (annual revenue $1M-$10M)
- **Enterprise OEM**: Custom pricing based on deployment scale

**Benefits**:
- All Standard Commercial License benefits
- Right to embed in commercial products
- Right to offer as managed service
- Technical integration support
- Custom branding options
- Dedicated account manager

### 5. ENTERPRISE GLOBAL LICENSE

**Purpose**: Comprehensive license for large multinational organizations.

**Pricing**: Custom pricing based on organization size, revenue, and deployment needs

**Benefits**:
- Unlimited deployments worldwide
- Full rights to customize and redistribute
- Dedicated support team
- Service Level Agreement (SLA)
- Strategic partnership benefits
- Influence on product roadmap
- Annual executive briefing

## REGIONAL PRICING ADJUSTMENTS

All pricing is subject to regional adjustments based on World Bank country classifications:

- **High-income economies**: 100% of listed price
- **Upper-middle-income economies**: 70% of listed price
- **Lower-middle-income economies**: 40% of listed price
- **Low-income economies**: 20% of listed price
