## When You Need a Commercial License

The AGPL-3.0 license requires that if you modify Mixcore CMS and make it available to users over a network, you must release your complete source code under the AGPL-3.0 license.

**You need a commercial license if you want to:**

- Use Mixcore CMS in proprietary/closed-source applications
- Distribute Mixcore CMS as part of a commercial product without source code disclosure
- Integrate Mixcore CMS into SaaS offerings without AGPL-3.0 obligations
- Remove AGPL-3.0 copyleft requirements for your deployments
- Use Mixcore trademarks in your product marketing (requires separate Trademark License)
- Receive priority support, SLAs, and dedicated assistance

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I use Mixcore CMS for free?

Yes! Mixcore CMS is free to use under the AGPL-3.0 license. You can use it for personal projects, open-source projects, and even commercial projects as long as you comply with AGPL-3.0 requirements (including source code disclosure).

### Do I need a commercial license for internal tools?

If your internal tools are not distributed or made available over a network to external users, you may not need a commercial license. However, if employees or contractors access the system remotely, AGPL-3.0 may apply. Contact us for clarification.

### What if I modify Mixcore CMS?

Under AGPL-3.0, modifications must be released under the same license if you distribute the software or make it available over a network. A commercial license removes this requirement.

### Can I switch from AGPL-3.0 to a commercial license?

Yes, you can purchase a commercial license at any time. The commercial license will apply to future use; past use under AGPL-3.0 is not affected.

### Do commercial licenses include the mix-heart submodule?

The `mix-heart` library is licensed under MIT and can be used freely in commercial projects. Commercial licenses for Mixcore CMS do not affect the mix-heart licensing.
