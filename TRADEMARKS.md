This document outlines the trademark policy for Mixcore CMS and related marks owned by the Mixcore Foundation.

### Logos and Design Marks

### Domain Names

## Purpose of This Policy

Our trademarks are important to us because they represent the quality and integrity of the Mixcore project. This policy aims to:

1. Clarify proper use of Mixcore trademarks
2. Protect users from confusion about the source of products
3. Enable community use while protecting brand integrity
4. Support the open-source community

## Permitted Uses (No License Required)

### Nominative Fair Use

You may use Mixcore trademarks without permission when:

- **Referring to the software**: "This application is built with Mixcore CMS"
- **Stating compatibility**: "Compatible with Mixcore CMS"
- **Community discussions**: Discussing, reviewing, or critiquing Mixcore
- **Educational content**: Teaching, writing articles, or creating tutorials about Mixcore
- **News and journalism**: Reporting about Mixcore in news articles or blog posts

### Requirements for Nominative Use

When using our trademarks nominatively:

- Use the trademark as an adjective, not a noun or verb
- Include the appropriate trademark symbol (™ or ®) on first use
- Do not alter the trademark spelling or design
- Include attribution: "Mixcore is a trademark of the Mixcore Foundation"

### Examples of Permitted Use

- "Powered by Mixcore CMS"
- "Built on Mixcore"
- "For use with Mixcore CMS"
- "An extension for Mixcore"
- "[Your Product] for Mixcore"

## Restricted Uses (License Required)

The following uses require explicit written permission:

### Commercial Use

- Using Mixcore trademarks in product names
- Using Mixcore logos in commercial materials
- Marketing products as "official" Mixcore products
- White-labeling or OEM distribution

### Domain Names

- Registering domain names containing "mixcore"
- Exception: Clearly unofficial community sites (e.g., "mixcore-community-forum.org")

### Merchandise

- Producing merchandise (t-shirts, stickers, etc.) with Mixcore trademarks for sale

### Modified Software

- Distributing modified versions of Mixcore using our trademarks
- You MUST rename modified versions to avoid confusion

## Prohibited Uses (Not Permitted)

The following uses are NOT permitted under any circumstances:

- Using trademarks in ways that suggest official endorsement without authorization
- Using trademarks in misleading, defamatory, or disparaging ways
- Registering trademarks that are confusingly similar to Mixcore marks
- Using trademarks in connection with illegal activities
- Creating logos confusingly similar to Mixcore logos
- Implying partnership, sponsorship, or endorsement without agreement

## Modified Versions

If you distribute a modified version of Mixcore CMS:

### Required

1. **Remove Mixcore trademarks** from your distribution
2. **Choose a different name** for your modified version
3. **Clearly indicate** that your version is modified and not official
4. **Include attribution** to the original Mixcore project

### Permitted

- Stating that your software is "based on Mixcore CMS"
- Stating that your software is "derived from Mixcore CMS"
- Linking to the official Mixcore website

### Example Acceptable Naming

- "[YourBrand] CMS (based on Mixcore)"
- "[YourBrand] Platform, powered by Mixcore technology"

### Example Unacceptable Naming

- "Mixcore [YourBrand] Edition"
- "[YourBrand] Mixcore"
- "Mixcore Plus"
- "Mixcore Pro"

## Logo Usage Guidelines

When using Mixcore logos (with permission):

### Do

- Use official logo files from our brand assets
- Maintain proper aspect ratios
- Provide adequate clear space around the logo
- Use appropriate color versions for the background

### Don't

- Modify the logo colors
- Add effects (shadows, gradients, outlines)
- Rotate or skew the logo
- Combine with other logos
- Place on busy backgrounds
- Recreate or redraw the logo

## Community Projects

We encourage community projects and provide additional flexibility:

### User Groups and Meetups

- May use "Mixcore" in group names (e.g., "Mixcore Developers NYC")
- Must not imply official Mixcore Foundation sponsorship
- Must be free to attend (or charge only to cover costs)

### Open Source Extensions/Plugins

- May indicate compatibility with Mixcore
- Should not use "Mixcore" as the primary product name
- Example: "SEO Tools for Mixcore" (acceptable)
- Example: "Mixcore SEO" (requires permission)

### Educational Content

- Courses, tutorials, and books may reference Mixcore
- May not imply official endorsement without permission
- Should include trademark attribution

## Requesting Permission

To request permission for trademark use:

1. Email: [trademarks@mixcore.org](mailto:trademarks@mixcore.org)
2. Include:
   - Description of intended use
   - Mockups or examples
   - Duration of use
   - Commercial or non-commercial purpose
   - Company/organization information

We aim to respond within 10 business days.
